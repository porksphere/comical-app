## Downloading a series

**Download** on a series page opens the chapter picker: everything, unread only, a range, or a
hand-picked set. Downloads run in the background and the series page shows each chapter's state —
queued, downloading, done.

A downloaded chapter reads exactly like an online one; the reader just doesn't go to the network.
Covers and the series' details are kept too, so the series page works without a connection.

## Auto-download

**Settings → General → Auto-download** keeps downloads happening without you:

| Toggle | What it downloads |
|---|---|
| **Saved series** | Every chapter of a series when you add it to the library |
| **New chapters** | Each new chapter of a saved series as Activity finds it |
| **Saved chapters** | A chapter when you bookmark it |
| **Saved pages** | A page when you save it |

**Download over Wi-Fi only** and **Download in background** are above these; on a phone the
background toggle lets the downloads continue after you leave the app.

## Managing space

The **Downloads** screen (from the Library tab) lists every downloaded series with its size.
Remove a chapter, a series, or all of it from there. On desktop the download location is under
**Settings → General → Data location**.

## Rate limits

Sources set their own limits on how fast they'll serve pages, and bridges tell the app what those
are. A download that's being held back shows as waiting rather than failing; it continues when the
source allows. **Settings → General → Show request activity** puts a small readout of what's
in flight, and what's being held, in the corner of the screen.
