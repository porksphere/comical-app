## Modes

- **Paged** — one page per screen, swipe or tap the sides to turn. For comics and manga.
- **Long strip** — pages flow vertically in one continuous scroll. For webtoons.

A series remembers its mode, so a webtoon stays a strip and a manga stays paged. The default for
new series is in the reader settings.

## Direction

Paged reading runs **left-to-right** or **right-to-left**. Right-to-left flips page turns, the
tap zones and the chapter keys together, so "next" is always toward the end of the chapter.

## Fit

Pages fit the screen whole by default. A **spread** — a page wider than it is tall — is shown at
full height instead and read by panning sideways, since squeezing it into the width would make it
a strip. You can also pin the fit to the width or the height for every page.

**Double-tap** switches the fit on the page under your finger: zooms in to fill the other axis
when there's room, out when the page overflows. The double-tap can instead be set to a plain zoom
toggle.

## Zoom and pan

Pinch to zoom; a zoomed page pans to its own edges and no further. Swiping past the edge of a
zoomed page turns to the next one. Pages are decoded at the size they're drawn, so large pages
don't cost memory until you zoom into them — **Downsample** in the reader settings turns that off
if you'd rather trade memory for sharpness.

## Controls

The reader's toolbar hides a few seconds after you open a chapter; tap the middle of the page to
bring it back. From it: the page slider, jump to the previous or next chapter, bookmark, and the
reader settings. On desktop, **Tab** shows the toolbar and focuses it — see
[Keyboard shortcuts](../keyboard-shortcuts/).

Swiping down on a page (or back on the series page) closes the reader with the page shrinking back
into the card it came from.

## Saving a page

**Save** in the toolbar — or **F** on desktop — keeps the current page in your collection of saved
pages, reachable from the Library tab.
