## Saving a series

**+ Library** on a series page keeps it. Saved series are what Activity watches for new chapters,
what auto-download fetches, and what sync carries between devices.

## Collections and groups

The **Library** tab opens on **All**. Collections are shelves you make — *Reading*, *Favorites*
and whatever else — and a series can sit on several. Hold a series (right-click on desktop) to
add it to a collection, or create one from the collection menu.

**Groups** are sections inside a collection: a collection of ongoing series might group by day of
the week, or by source. Drag to reorder either.

## Filtering and sorting

The filter button on any shelf narrows it by source, status or unread state, and sorts by last
read, last updated, or title. The filter is remembered per shelf.

## Reading history

The **History** tab lists every chapter you've opened, newest first, and is where **Resume** gets
its place from. Clear a row with a swipe.

## Favorites on a source

Separate from your library: a bridge that supports favorites — because its backend has an account
with a list — exposes that list under its source, and can import it into your library in one go.

## Backup

**Settings → General → Library backup** exports the library, its collections and your progress to
a file, and restores from one. On a phone that runs bridges itself the library lives on the
device, so this is the thing to do before changing phones. (If you also run the desktop app,
[sync](../sync/) keeps a copy there continuously.)
