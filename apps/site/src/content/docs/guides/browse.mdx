## Sources

The **Browse** tab shows one source — one installed bridge — at a time. The title at the top is a
menu of every source you have; tap it to switch. On desktop the sources are the sidebar.

A source opens on its **home**: the lists the bridge chose to feature, each a rail you can expand
with **Show all**. Some bridges offer more lists than fit on home; those are under the second title
menu next to the source's name.

## Search

The search button searches the source you're looking at. Searching from the top-level search (or
**Ctrl/⌘ K** on desktop) runs the query across **every** source at once and groups the results by
source.

Bridges that support it expose **filters** — genre, status, sort order — under the filter button
on a results page or a list. Which filters exist is up to the bridge.

## Series badges

Cards carry two badges: **NEW** for a series whose latest chapter is recent, and **HOT** for one a
source marks as trending. A number in the corner is unread chapters, for series in your library.

## Adult content

**Settings → General → NSFW content** sets whether bridges marked adult are shown, hidden, or hidden
until unlocked. Hidden sources disappear from Browse and search entirely.
