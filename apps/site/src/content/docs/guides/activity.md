## The feed

The **Activity** tab is a feed of new chapters across every series in your library, newest first,
grouped by series. Tap a row to open the chapter. It also records when a source removes a series
you'd saved, so a disappearance isn't a mystery.

## The badge

The number on the Activity tab is the count of **unread entries in the feed** — not of unread
chapters. It goes down when you read, when you swipe a row and mark it read, or when you clear the
feed. It doesn't go down just because you looked at the tab.

## Checking for new chapters

**Settings → Notifications**:

- **Check for new chapters** — Comical checks your saved series each time it opens.
- **Check in background** — on a phone, it keeps checking while closed, as often as the OS allows.
- **Wi-Fi only** — hold background checks until there's Wi-Fi.
- **Only for series I'm caught up on** — series you're behind on stay out of the feed until you
  catch up, so the feed is things to read *now*.

## Notifications

**Notify about new chapters** sends a notification per check that finds something; **App icon
badge** mirrors the unread count onto the app icon. Both are phone-only.

Pair with **Auto-download → New chapters** ([Downloads](../downloads/)) and the chapter is waiting
on the device by the time you tap the notification.
